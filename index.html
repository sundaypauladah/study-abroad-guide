A Note from Sunday Paul Adah — Founder & CEO, Radius

In 2014, I almost lost my admission to a US university because my tuition payment didn't arrive on time. I wasn't broke — the money existed, but the international payment system failed me. That experience became the founding force behind Radius. If a guide like this had existed, I could have found other funding options while waiting for my funds to clear.

This guide exists because too many current and potential international students are missing out on opportunities for global quality education. Some believe studying abroad is too expensive. Others are paying for information that should be free. Scholarship lists, country guides, application advice: students deserve access to all of it simply because they want to study abroad, not because they can afford to pay for it.

Everything in this guide is free. 34 countries. 140+ universities. 60+ scholarships and grants. 12 student loan providers. Verified deadlines and requirements. No paywall, no email required to browse.

Use it. Share it. And if you ever need to pay tuition and fees internationally without FX delays, that's what Radius is for. You can also download the app to join a growing community of over 120,000 international students around the world — and refer your friends to earn from every transaction they complete on Radius.

I'd love to see you onboard someday. Feel free to connect with me on the Radius community or across my various social media accounts by searching for my full name below.

Cheers!

Sunday Paul Adah — Founder & CEO, Radius

Once you have your offer, Radius handles what comes next
Most students lose their admission not because of grades — because of delayed tuition payments, blocked FX transfers, or no credit history abroad. Radius was built by an international student who nearly lost his admission for exactly this reason. It's the solution we wish existed.
💰
Tuition & Fee Payments
Pay tuition to universities in Germany, UK, Canada, and 30+ countries in minutes, not weeks. Same-day processing at competitive exchange rates with real-time FX calculator.
📄
SEVIS, WES & Visa Fees
Pay SEVIS, WES credential evaluation, application fees, visa fees, and embassy payments all from one platform without bank wire hassles.
🏠
Deposits & Housing Payments
Pay accommodation deposits and housing fees internationally. Know exactly where your money is at every step — no guesswork, just peace of mind.

Credit Building
